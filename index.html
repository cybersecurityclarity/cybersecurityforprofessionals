Cybersecurity Bootcamp for Professionals

Course Overview

The Cybersecurity Bootcamp for Professionals is a comprehensive, hands-on program designed to develop job-ready cyber talent equipped to operate in real-world, regulated environments. Participants build core capabilities across networking, Linux and Windows security, virtualization, applied cryptography, threat detection, incident response, and AI-augmented security operations — with training scenarios aligned to real banking environments (fraud cases, business email compromise, wire-transfer attacks, and emerging AI-driven threats).

  • Practical Experience: ~70% of the course is dedicated to labs and simulations.
  • Role-Based Learning: Team-based labs assign roles such as Security Analyst, Network Engineer, and Incident Commander.
  • Adversarial Mindset: Participants learn to think like threat actors to better anticipate and mitigate risk.
  • AI Integration: Hands-on work with tools like Microsoft Security Copilot and Power BI Copilot under responsible-AI guardrails.

Duration: 10 weeks (not inclusive of optional professional business-skills modules)
